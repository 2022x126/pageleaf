How to use it

  1. Open an article

    Open a web article in desktop Chrome. Click the Pageleaf toolbar icon.

  2. Mark and write

    Highlight or underline text, draw with the pen, and write in My notes.

  3. Save your changes

    Click Save to keep your notes and marks. Export a file when you need a separate copy.

Open the help center ↗

Where your notes are saved

Your notes stay in the current Chrome profile. Pageleaf does not upload articles or notes. Back up before uninstalling or changing devices.
