Quick help

  1. Open a web article, or select the text you want to keep.
  2. Click Pageleaf in the Chrome toolbar, or press Alt/Option + Shift + L (if available).
  3. Add a note, save locally, or export a file.

Pin Pageleaf from Chrome’s extensions menu. PDFs, internal browser pages, extension stores and some app pages cannot be captured.

Unsaved captures are temporary and disappear when the browser closes. Saved notes stay in this Chrome profile, unencrypted. Images are not loaded and article content is not uploaded.

Search covers titles, article text, sources and personal notes. Back up downloads a JSON copy of saved notes. Restore adds new notes and skips duplicates. Keep backup files private.

Saved notes remember your reading position. Print / PDF opens the browser print dialog, where you can save a PDF. Unsaved personal notes are included.

Uninstalling the extension deletes its local notes. Back up regularly.
